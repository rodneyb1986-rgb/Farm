The business model

You sell. Supplier fulfills.

01

Customer buys from you

Customer sees your brand, your product page and your retail price and pays your checkout.

02

Order is routed

Your backend sends the order to the authorized supplier at your agreed wholesale cost, including the customer's shipping details.

03

Supplier ships

Supplier ships directly to the customer and returns tracking. Your system sends the customer the tracking notification.

Example margin: If your negotiated supplier cost is $1,000 and you sell for $1,299, your gross product margin is $299 before payment fees, advertising, refunds, taxes and any shipping difference. Never assume the public Hayspear price is your wholesale cost.
